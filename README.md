# Urinary-Bladder-Cancer-Treatment-in-India
Urinary bladder cancer requires timely diagnosis, appropriate treatment planning, and regular medical monitoring. Depending on the stage and severity, treatment may involve TURBT, chemotherapy, immunotherapy, radiation therapy, or bladder removal surgery.
India offers various treatment options for international patients seeking specialised cancer care. Understanding the available procedures, expected expenses, and recovery requirements can help patients make informed decisions.
